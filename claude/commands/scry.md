# /scry

Where are we? Answer in this shape, one line per item, nothing else:

- **Needs me:** each decision waiting on me, with your pick.
- **In progress:** what's running, and what it waits on.
- **Done since I last asked:** what landed, with links.

Leave out a heading that has nothing under it.
