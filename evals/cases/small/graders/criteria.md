The agent goes ahead with the build as the owner chose. It writes no spec, plan document or requirements list, and asks nothing the owner must answer before it starts. It may add at most one short sentence offering something extra, which the owner can take or leave. A reply fails if it writes a spec or plan, waits for answers, switches to a heavier process than the owner chose, or presses an extra step more than once.
