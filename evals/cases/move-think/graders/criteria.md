Before building, the agent gets the intent clear: it asks a few short questions (what feels slow, where, measured how, what may be stale) or proposes a quick measurement or throwaway prototype to find out, and does not commit to a cache design yet. A reply that picks a cache layer, tool or design and says it will build it, without first finding out what is slow, fails.
