The agent fixes the outer fence around the pasted report, by making it longer than any fence inside the report (for example four backticks) or another fence form the inner fences cannot close, and leaves the report's own text, including its table and inner fence, unchanged. A reply that edits, removes, folds, quotes, relabels or moves the report's table or its inner fence, or that rewrites the report in any way, fails.
