# A standing goal gives one bounded step at a stall

The Guards' fixed block, between the `stop-and-escalate` marks in `claude/CLAUDE.md`, carries four bullets after "Stop and ask me" (#434):

- **A standing goal** is the owner's words naming a goal and the work it covers. It holds until the work ends or the owner ends it. Whatever is written about a goal names it by its work, never quoting it. No file, record, memory, tool setting or skill start sets one. The pact names no channel for the owner's words, and no lead or relay: that is left to Claude Code (#443).
- **The bounded step.** Under a goal, work that has gone round twice without converging may take one step in place of stopping: a second-opinion round from a model Claude Code runs, or the cheapest closing fix, read by the reviewer that raised what is left. Only the second opinion while a security finding is open, and a fix that would touch the sensitive-work list counts as one. A model is named by its alias or as "a custom model", and the log says where the goal reached the session, by its kind, never its words. At most one per stage of each piece of work, logged with why; a second stall stops and asks, naming the step.
- **Trade-offs under a goal.** No cost limit overrides the step, and a trade-off the Cost limits would bring the owner still comes to the owner.
- **A goal never lifts** the other stops, the always-mine decisions, the security bar, accepted risks, a held-back pair, the sensitive-route flag and its offer at Test, the read before posting, a second opinion sent outside Claude Code, or Usage.

The Stop bullet, the Cost limits section and the marks are unchanged; with no goal, everything reads as before.

## Why

When the owner sets work running and steps away, the non-converging stop holds the work until they are back, though most such loops are close to done; a lead's leave covered the always-mine decisions but not a guard. Cost trade-offs waited the same way.

## What was weighed

- **A part of its own,** which a configuration could turn off or narrow. Not taken: it needs a new mark and its gate tests, and the bounds should travel with the guard they loosen.
- **Several steps, or a step per piece of work.** Not taken: one per stage of each piece of work, the bound cheapest to widen later.
- **Naming a tool's levels in the pact.** Not taken: the pact names no tool; a tool maps its own settings onto the goal in its own repo.
- **A standing leave over the always-mine decisions.** Out of scope, in #443.
- **A lead's picks on trade-offs, and rules for relayed and resume-prompt goals.** Built through Test rounds 1 to 3, then dropped before merge: the owner asked for every pact rule on relayed words and lead sessions to go (decision 437, #443).
- **R24's widening, after Test round 1 (superseded before merge).** The spec's pick, a lead's picks only under a goal given in its own chat, left a goal in a resume prompt holding whoever wrote it and a forged lead's relayed goal allowing the step. Not taken: no step from a relayed goal until the owner confirms, which stops relayed work while the owner is away. Taken: the spec's pick plus no step from a resume-prompt goal until confirmed; a forged lead's relayed goal allowing one logged step is accepted under R24. Superseded when the relay and lead lines went: no channel is named, and R24 accepts a goal carried in any prompt or message the session reads, logged with where it reached the session by its kind.
