# Review bookkeeping keeps only what feeds a decision

The pact's review bookkeeping is cut to what the owner's decisions used:

- **The thorough pick is gone** (#164). The pact no longer asks the owner where they expect the problem before the verdict, at any tier. The cross script's `pick` mode is deleted, with its tests, and its sections and page no longer prompt for a pick. The thorough tier still folds the verdict below the cards and the map.
- **The Lens dispositions table keeps five things:** the finding, the lens, the disposition with its `auto` mark, the cross result, and "override, not security-tested" in the rows of a lens the configuration notice marks. The crossing-real, changed-my-decision, time, model and effort, and configuration columns are gone, and so are the pre-fills and their confirmation round.
- **"When a lens may not pay" and "Totals only" are deleted.** No session proposes a lens review on a signal, and no session collects totals into this repo's tracker. A periodic review is now the owner reading the disposition tables when they choose.
- **Two sentences stay.** The cross-project rule becomes its own paragraph, "Across projects": a review that reads several projects copies no repo names, links, issue numbers, titles, headlines, anchors, paths or quotes from one into another, and no per-project breakdown. The public-repo escape sentence stays word for word.
- **Escapes and roster gaps stay,** and count only when the owner confirms them.
- **The lens contracts match.** Their pick lines, their "Changed my decision?" lines and their "rarely changes the decision" signals are gone; "Record and review" now says the owner reads the tables when they choose.
- **The threat model retires R18,** "your review totals go to this repo's tracker". Its row stays, marked retired, so the other labels keep their numbers.

## Why

- **The pick was skipped or answered "none" each time,** and fed no decision on record (#164).
- **The columns cost a round at every review.** Only the dispositions and the `auto` marks fed a decision.
- **The signals never fired.** One periodic review was run (#35, 12 reviews). It kept every lens, no "may not pay" signal fired, and none of 73 auto-takes was reversed.
- **Totals to this repo were a flow out of every project** that used the pact, R18, with nothing on record that needed it.
