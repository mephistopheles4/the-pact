# Review rounds stop at two a stage

#431. The pact's default Cost limits gain one row, Review rounds: at most two rounds at one stage, a spec or Test, follow-ups and closing reads included, the second declared final. A finding past the limit goes the row's At the limit way: a new low is noted in the review's record or filed, a new medium becomes a trade-off, a high security finding reopens the stage. The row is the owner's installed row word for word; its only authority is that row, and nothing else is copied from the owner's installed file. Decisions 418, 421, 432, 444, 458 and 465 were the goal holder's at level 3, not the owner's words.

## What changed

- **The row** joins `claude/CLAUDE.md` after Lens pairs and `DEFAULT_PARTS.costLimits` in `builder/parts.mjs`, so the default parts still compile byte for byte to the rules file. Ultra Code stays uncapped (0101).
- **Three edits in `claude/review.md`** ("Handling the reports") carry the readings below. The rules file has 3 words of budget left (2,247 of 2,250), so the readings live where every lens run and every post is read first, once installed.
- **The round title line** takes `[, reopened]` and `[, final]` marks; a further read outside the count is titled `## <stage> further read on ...`. The status comment carries the same marks. Nothing in the gate or the poster parses a title line.

## The readings

- **A count bounds rounds, never lenses.** It limits how many rounds run, not which lenses run in one. The Lens pairs row and the Cost limits opening hold as before.
- **D1: a read the cap would hold back still runs.** If the security pair, a repo's required lenses or a typed skill's lenses would read anything after a stage's final round, that read runs as a further read, outside the count, with the Guards' "gone round twice" as its brake. The stage closes, and nothing is accepted or merged, only once every further read has reported. So the row holds back none of the three lens kinds 0101 says a cost never holds back.
- **D3: a high security finding reopens the stage and its count starts again.** Its next round is `round 1, reopened` and runs the security pair. Nothing else resets the count.
- **D4: the security bar holds at any severity.** A security or `security?` finding past the limit, low or medium, keeps the security bar and is never routed as a trade-off.
- **Reruns.** A lens that errors, returns no report or comes back inconclusive runs again inside its round; a rerun is not a new round. Every report it returned stays in the record. A withheld report stays local: its text appears in no post, yet its findings still count, and a high security finding among them reopens the stage. A withheld read has reported once the owner has answered it in chat. A report the poster refused is never rerun.
- **A ticket cut from an approved spec gets no spec round of its own;** a ticket that changes the spec gets its own rounds.

## Why

On 2026-10-27 a lead ran six sensitive-route tickets at once and counted about 115 lens runs in a day. Early rounds found real defects. Rounds after the second closing read mostly found lows in text the previous fix had just written, and each security finding in them reached the owner as an accepted-risk record, about fifteen in the day. The only brake was the Guards' "gone round twice", which fires after the fact.

## What was weighed

- **Separate spec and Test limits (the issue's first form).** Not taken: the owner asked for one limit for both stages.
- **Putting the readings in the row.** Not taken: the row matches the owner's installed row word for word, and the budget leaves 3 words.
- **Holding back the security pair past the limit.** Not taken: 0101 says a cost never holds back the security pair, a repo's required lenses or a typed skill's lenses; D1 keeps that.

## What stays the same

The Guards, the protected set, the other cost rows and `docs/threat-model.md` are unchanged. The guard and the row overlap at two by design: the guard is a judgment over any loop and stops to ask; the row is a count over review rounds and routes findings without a stop.

## Accepted risks

Under a goal, the goal holder's answer frees a withheld read's close gate; this fits the threat model's "a standing goal decides alone" (P2). R6 and R7 from the final round on spec r4, and S2 from the further read on r5, were accepted by the goal holder, nothing built.

Narrows 0101 in nothing. Until the owner installs the pact, sessions read the installed `review.md` and run without these readings.
