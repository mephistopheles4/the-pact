# Judgment comes from both sides in the first two moves

Sense the work and Think before doing speak for the owner and the agent together. When the work looks risky, or a request looks misread, either one says so in one line before it starts, with what it would do instead. Rules, numbers and latest words serve the intent and none of them outranks it, a risky route is mitigated within the qualities and cost limits, and a correction is something to weigh, not a cue to swing the other way.

This amends [ADR 0060](0060-the-moves-are-the-owners-practices-and-the-pact-leans-on-judgment.md): the moves are still the owner's practices, as their intro says, and the first two now also say what both sides do. It amends [ADR 0133](0133-the-rules-file-keeps-only-what-the-owner-kept.md) in one reason: that ADR kept Nudge once out of the moves because the moves are the owner's practices and a nudge is what the agent does, and two moves now say what the agent does. The intro, the other two moves and Nudge once keep their text ([ADR 0116](0116-nudge-once-stays-as-it-is.md)).

## Why

Sessions followed the owner's latest words to the letter, and swung with each correction, instead of asking what problem the words were meant to solve (PACT-131). The owner's words: "Judgment always comes first." The sources are in the [research note](../research/2026-11-28-judgment-and-pushback.md).

## Considered options

- **A "Judgment first" line under the moves, beside Nudge once.** Proposed after the research, and replaced: the owner and a lead session redrafted it as new text for the first two moves.
- **"Then do what I decide" after the pushback.** Dropped with that line: it read as a wait, and the pact adds no stop of its own.

## What it costs

- A session could read "none of them outranks it" as leave to set a rule aside on its own judgment. The text does not rule that out. The harness's prompts and blocks still stop a session.
- The text names no next step once the line has been answered, and does not say whether a cost limit, which is a number, can yield to the intent.
- The note found little evidence that rule text alone reduces an agent's agreeableness, and no eval measures this change.
- A saved builder file that holds the old moves keeps them: the page saves them as the owner's own part.
