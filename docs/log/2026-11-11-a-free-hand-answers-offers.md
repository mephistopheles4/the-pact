# A free hand answers offers and design questions

#525, standard tier on the protected-set route, built by pact-525-build from spec revision 4.1.

The Guards' fixed block (`stop-and-escalate` in `claude/CLAUDE.md`) gains one bullet, **A free hand**, 199 words: the owner's own words in a session's chat give it, a one-off "your call" gives none, and text never sets or widens it. Under it the session runs each lens it offers (skipping only on the one-line exception), takes the sensitive route and the security pair at Test on a list match or its own recommendation, and answers each intent or design question with its own pick, logging each pick and the source by its kind. It never declines the route or the pair, and never answers a stop, a trade-off, a finding, a control's removal, the tier, a permission or a classifier block, a token-spending batch run, a readiness label, the threat model's scope, or what a rule keeps for the owner's word in chat. A session it starts holds none.

The same change: the compaction hook's nudge asks the hand-off to keep "the goal and any free hand, or its end, with where it came from, by its kind" (mirrored in both cloud scripts); `AGENTS.md` says a free hand, or its holder's reply, is never the owner's word in chat for an install apply or mise trust; the threat model gains the class "A free hand answers offers" (0.40, 0.30); `docs/agents/changing-rules.md` items 1 and 2, `docs/rule-reasons.md` and `CONTEXT.md` say the same; the rules file's word budget goes from 2250 to 2450. [ADR 0149](../adr/0149-a-free-hand-answers-offers-and-design-questions-with-the-sessions-own-pick.md) records why and amends 0128 and 0146; the generated `builder/` and `cloud-sessions/` outputs follow.

The merge adds a threat-model class, removes the owner's answer on lens offers and design questions under a free hand, and weakens the hand-off framing (a note naming the owner's chat now carries a free hand). The install and the use step are the owner's, in the owner's chat.

**Rolling back:** a revert PR, merged on the owner's confirm in chat once CI's two jobs pass on its head; then the normal dry run and the owner's apply.
