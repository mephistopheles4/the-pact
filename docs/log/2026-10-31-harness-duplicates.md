# The harness duplicates dropped, and a budget so they don't grow back

#450, standard tier on Opus at high, specified and built by do-450, led by the headchef. The owner's ask and its widenings came by relay; the spec go on revision 3 was the lead's own pick under the owner's level-3 command, not the owner's words.

The scan read the rules file, the review procedure and the agents against Claude Code's docs and what a session is told, and against the pact's own tools. It cut the read-only line in five lenses and `scout` and the half-context hand-off, kept and named the lines that overlap but cover a gap of their own, and added a word budget, a reasons table and the threat-model revision (ADR 0129).

Design ran two rounds. Round 1, the spec pair on revision 1, found 16 findings, among them no Needs a human section, two decisions left for build time, the sign-in sentence's stop, and `scout` missed; revision 2 fixed or took them all. Round 2, the final one, added the security pair on the revision rule (decision 457): 21 findings, none high from the security pair, and one high stall for rules a rebase adds. Revision 3 answered each, on the lead's picks for the security rows (decision 461). Both security lenses also found that seam A no longer checks an agent's tools, so `scout`'s contract no longer says it does.

The removal of "accepted risks only on my word" went to #443 (decision 452).

The build was rebased onto #434 and #443. #434's four new Guards rules got reasons rows (decision 469), and the budgets were measured again by the spec's rule. Test round 1 ran the security pair's full read of the squashed local commit, which released the push, and the QA pair. Its fixes widened the revision bullet: a re-read keeps what a fold or a drop keeps and raises a risk it finds grown, a future-dated line counts as none, drops rest on the scope as it stood before the revision, and a declined proposal waits a month. That pushed the rules file past its budget, which rose to 4,000 as a spec change on the lead's pick (decision 488). The threat model's R70 now names the revision. Test round 2, the final round, added the standards pair as a third pair (decision 483). Nothing blocked; its leftovers went into one more fix commit on the lead's pick (decision 491): a decline counts when given in chat and is noted with its date, a change that writes the `Last revised:` line other than a revision is a finding, a grown risk is raised in full, the reasons rows each name their gap, and ADR 0129 says it amends 0056. The security pair read that commit in full before its push, past the round limit, on the same pick.
